We acknowledge the **Osteotheque de Pessac, Universite de Bordeaux, France**,
which curates the physical specimen, and **Dr Stephane Rottier**, curator.
The source item's bundled readme specifies CC BY-NC 4.0, citation and
acknowledgment requirements, and curator notification when publishing a
scientific article involving the specimen. Preserve those terms for future
reuse and manuscript preparation. No notification or journal submission was
performed by this analysis.

Changes: applied the supplied shared rigid pose; computed central-plane
surface intersections, contour-gap summaries and deliberately shifted EDJ
contours; generated an annotated section figure. These operations did not
alter or validate a living tooth. Raw PLY surfaces are not redistributed.
Source sampling, smoothing, unknown segmentation error, open crown
boundaries and one zero-area EDJ face limit interpretation. See the
[analysis report](../../PAIRED_TOOTH_SURFACE_REFERENCE.md).
