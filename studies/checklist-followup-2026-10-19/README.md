## Findings that should affect the next experiment

**Dental:** the remineralized apparent indentation-toughness mean is 49.89%
above the acid-etched group and 10.76% below native. The enamel wear-volume
mean is 19.94% higher than native in the selected source block. These are
descriptive means, not equivalence, significance or clinical durability
results. Two unexplained dentin-volume entries stay unidentified. Repeated
indentations cannot increase the independent sample count.

**Aging:** the primary metadata-defined analysis uses ten healthy women with
batch adjustment because the cohort has only two healthy men, both young.
Structural ECM declines by 0.246 mean-log1p(CPM) units per decade, with every
primary donor omission negative. This is relative bulk expression. It does
not identify a cell-intrinsic mechanism or determine how to rejuvenate tissue.
A separately labelled post-hoc alias sensitivity retains the original gene
panel and also preserves the negative inflammation direction.

**Engineered tissue:** mean insulin stimulation index is 2.631 in the
flow/vascular-bed condition versus 1.442 in conventional wells. Its absolute
high-glucose insulin concentration is not the highest group mean. Spheroid,
donor, cell-lot and chip/batch units cannot be interchanged, and the workbook
does not supply those independent-unit crosswalks. The positive source result
is a useful functional chassis comparison, not dental or whole-organ repair.

**Exposure:** an HTTP-success response from a publisher endpoint contained
bibliographic metadata only. It cannot become a numerical PK curve. The
eligibility checks also reject false individual-curve pairing and an
intraperitoneal-only reference for absolute oral availability.

## Next concrete decisions

1. **Connect actual material depth to actual interface performance.** The
   source property values qualify a thin-layer comparison, not bulk repair.
   The next input must link each sample's measured depth/substrate to its
   crack path, leakage and wear/fatigue response, with process and native
   controls. Resolve source hierarchy before inferential uncertainty.
2. **Preserve the full natural-tooth endpoint.** Transfer a qualified hard-tissue
   architecture into the bilateral-insertion attachment comparison with its
   final crown/load geometry. Pulp, gums, socket, mobility, intended pose and
   stability after finite support withdrawal remain required. No source
   workbook or expression score satisfies those endpoints.
3. **Resolve cell state and cohort transport.** Prioritize a healthy,
   cell-resolved oral dataset with real donor-to-cell mapping across older
   women and men. The screened pooled libraries cannot be promoted to donors.
   More robust within-cohort fits do not settle between-cohort disagreement.
4. **Obtain joint functional construct observations.** Donor/lot, spheroid and
   circuit identifiers plus perfusion, identity, viability and function on a
   comparable sampling frame would let the earlier joint-endpoint audit do
   useful work on real constructs. Current separate measurements cannot
   establish joint success or genomic correction.
5. **Keep the exposure question quantitative.** An inspectable Figure 2B
   table or calibrated figure is needed before 915 PK integration. Source
   route/timing conflicts and assay/sample hierarchy must remain visible.
   SS-31, MOTS-c, capped peptides, caffeine reversal and reproductive selectivity
   remain in the [previous decision reports](../checklist-progress-2026-10-19/README.md).
