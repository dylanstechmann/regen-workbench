## Decisions supported now

For enamel/dentin restoration, distinguish a material's bulk resistance from
its attachment to the retained tooth. The next useful comparison must connect
actual regional thickness and geometry, host-interface behavior and damage
through loading on the same physical specimen. Keep earlier unfavorable wear,
low loads, missing measurements and identity conflicts. The existing
[measurement packet and linkage analysis](../checklist-mechanisms-2026-10-19/dental/REPORT.md)
can receive eligible records; it should not be populated with invented links.

For the periodontal attachment, qualify geometry-normalized passive mechanics,
both insertion interfaces and measured recovery together. Donor age and niche
need independent variation before a rejuvenation interpretation is possible.
The new aggregate benchmarks help expose measurement mismatches; they do not
provide a universal healthy modulus, a construct-to-native conversion or a
clinical acceptance threshold. Original position, gums, pulp, sensation and
durability after finite support withdrawal remain separate required outcomes.

For MOTS-c, resolve the identity of the measured molecular species and the
time course before ranking persistence modifications. Preserved metabolic
activity cannot substitute for repair function: the earlier MSC study's
unfavorable repair result remains relevant. Missing public donor-linked
endpoint data are retained as missing, rather than reconstructed from plots.
