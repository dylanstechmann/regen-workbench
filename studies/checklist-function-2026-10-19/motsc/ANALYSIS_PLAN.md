# MOTS-c function and intact-parent evidence: bounded exploratory plan

Written after the source-access and prior-work audit, before final calculations. This is not a preregistration, new peptide, treatment plan, or biological replication.

The existing atlas already distinguished K14Q pump/ELISA apparent clearance from human half-life and function; it also reviewed CK2 binding and tagged nuclear trafficking. The earlier checklist added the MSC metabolic-activation/repair counterexample. This batch must add source-data qualification and analytical specificity, rather than repeat that review.

1. Inspect the current MSC primary XML/HTML and linked supplement/data statement for numerical metabolism/repair observations and donor/culture/animal crosswalks. Keep Table 1 demographic aggregates separate from endpoint source data. Do not digitize images to invent donor pairing or reanalysis.
2. If numerical endpoint data remain on request, freeze their unavailable state and pivot to primary intact-parent analytical/PK eligibility. Preserve twelve human donors, reported culture groups, recipient mice, repeated MRI visits and nested histology fields as different units. No field, well, visit, kidney compartment or donor/mouse total becomes an independent combined sample size.
3. Qualify the Knoop LC/MS versus ELISA comparison using the primary indexed abstract and publisher-visible supporting-information captions. Distinguish a detection limit from quantification, reference endogenous samples from administered exposure, and kit-standard identity from true patient analyte identity. Retain access failures; do not claim unseen spectra or numerical stability curves.
4. Calculate only the unit-converted ratio of the reported ELISA concentration range to the reported LC/MS LLOD. This is an analytical scale separation, not assay bias, a concentration bound for every person, absence of endogenous peptide, or in-vivo PK. Participant-level paired values are absent.
5. Recheck K14Q experimental context: single pump/steady-state ELISA sampling, variant-specific calibration and species/matrix, rather than a serial intact-parent decay curve. No rate constant, half-life, oral bioavailability or absolute native-parent clearance is estimated from it.
6. Implement source-aware gates for numerical donor-linked repair comparison, native-parent systemic PK and oral F. Unknowns remain missing. Only an independently identified intact-parent concentration-time dataset with suitable sampling, baseline handling, matrix calibration and route context can pass a quantitative PK gate. Absolute clearance from AUC additionally needs known parent input and complete parent AUC or qualified extrapolation; absolute oral F needs dose-normalized paired oral/IV parent AUCs with explicit complete or qualified extrapolated coverage for both routes. Two finite-window AUCs cannot pass that absolute-F gate. A passing source-presence gate would still not itself compute benefit or causation.
7. Reproduce bounded arithmetic and test invalid units/values, absent donor linkage, detection-versus-quantification, endogenous-versus-administered context, and immunoreactivity-versus-parent distinctions. Downloaded raw sources stay ignored with genuine `regen.record` receipts; no recipes, new sequences or source figures are redistributed.
