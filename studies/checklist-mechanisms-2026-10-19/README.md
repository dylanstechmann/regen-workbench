## Findings that change the next step

**Dental:** the next useful evidence must link actual repair depth/substrate
to actual crack/interface, leakage and fatigue outcomes at specimen level.
The new synthetic linked examples exercise that analysis; they do not fill
missing biological observations in the published material workbook.

**Aging:** observed and common-RNA partial mixtures retain positive structural
ECM age slopes (+0.213 and +0.183 per decade), opposing the earlier bulk
direction. The retained groups cover 99.55% of captured cells, but omitted
RNA is unknown. This is a partial captured-cell calculation, not reconstructed
whole gingiva. Neither this standardization nor more within-cohort robustness
establishes a universal youthful expression target.

**SS-31:** at unchanged parent clearance, matching SC systemic parent AUC
requires an oral-to-SC assayed-parent material ratio of `0.92 / oral F`.
With oral F unknown, its mathematical lower bound is 0.92 and it has no
uniform finite upper bound. This is material accounting, not a cost, duration
or intracellular-benefit prediction. The source-cell endpoint audit also
retains repeated normalized values across worksheets as unresolved dependence.

**Caffeine:** in the explicitly chosen 10 µM buffer case, central measured
affinity requires 367.57 µM total WP6 for 90% capture without competitors.
Equimolar total host captures 17.21%. These are mathematical concentrations,
not human exposure or dose estimates. Competition and finite capacity make
the earlier ideal capture scenario much more demanding.

   repair-depth uncertainty, host/repair crack path, leakage detection limits
   and fatigue censoring. Retain unfavorable and missing outcomes. The bridge
   can analyze these inputs without inventing specimen pairing.

   bilateral cementum–PDL–bone insertion, pulp/sensation, gum architecture and
   intended position after finite support ends. Shape or material repair alone
   cannot satisfy the full natural-tooth goal.
3. Prefer matched-donor bulk/cell-resolved gingiva with qualified site, health,
   age/sex coverage and independent functional measurements over choosing
   whichever age signature looks favorable. The current disagreement remains.
4. Keep parent-specific PK, useful cellular function and manufacturing recovery
   separate for SS-31. A chemical modification cannot inherit the parent's
   distribution, elimination or activity without measurements.
5. For caffeine capture, qualify physiological-matrix free-guest binding,
   available intact host, active-metabolite coverage and exchange kinetics
   before asking whether oral delivery could restore sleep. A different
   stimulant/host pair is only a mechanism precedent.
