# SS-31: useful exposure and released-material decision audit

This is an exploratory source-data reanalysis, written after inspecting the sources and before producing the final calculations. It is not a preregistration. Scope: unchanged-parent route/process improvement versus the evidence needed to justify a covalent alteration. No new analogue, preparation method, or human-use regimen is proposed.

1. Pin the earlier atlas audit, distinguish repeated evidence from new quantitative qualification, and preserve the exact analyte, route, population, unit and reported statistic for each primary input.
2. Keep human plasma incubation half-lives, IV residence time, SC terminal half-life and intracellular function as different outcomes. Do not divide their half-lives to infer a clearance mechanism. Do not count metabolite exposure as intact-parent exposure.
3. Use the FDA review's rounded normal-renal IV urinary parent recovery (~0.10; six subjects; collection through 48 h) only as a conditional sensitivity anchor. A clearance-partition calculation requires adequate collection, linear time-invariant disposition, urine containing the measured unchanged parent, and fixed unchanged-parent renal clearance. The counterfactual also holds distribution volume fixed; no terminal half-life is predicted. Other parent disappearance may include renal metabolism as well as processes elsewhere. It is not identified as plasma proteolysis or nonrenal clearance.
4. For unchanged parent at unchanged clearance, bound oral material needed to match SC systemic parent AUC using the label's approximately 0.92 SC absolute bioavailability and only the mathematical constraint 0 < oral F <= 1. Do not assign an oral F. Keep target access, function and finished-material unit cost unknown. Covalent comparators are ineligible for a numerical benefit ranking unless these additional quantities are measured.
5. Reconstruct Figure 6 experiment/sample summaries from the actual primary workbook. Use the three experiment-level TMRM entries, not microscopy fields as independent replicates. Report descriptive means/SD and source sample counts, no new p-values. These are normalized endpoint data, not measured intact intracellular concentrations. Record repeated values across worksheets without asserting their cause or independent replication.
6. Treat patent quantities as applicant assertions. Separate HPLC area purity, weight assay, final-parent identity/salt, isolated mass, molar yield, released batch output and cost. Do not infer overall parent yield or commercial cost from precursor/output mass or intermediate charge tables. Calculate only an explicitly intermediate-specific area-versus-weight-assay accounting example.
7. Test mechanism-specific rejection gates, unknown oral/cost/uptake handling, the conditional bound, and reconstruction of published experiment/sample means. Save deterministic results and source hashes; do not create a synthetic concentration-time curve.

Decision priority: obtain an analytically measured native-parent intracellular exposure/function relationship and an assayed, released-parent process mass balance before claiming that greater plasma stability, a different salt, or a purification change improves useful exposure per cost.
