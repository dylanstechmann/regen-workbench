## What the new data actually discriminate

**Dental:** the deposited G1/T11 fracture load is literally 6.76 N. Keeping it
gives a different group summary; omitting it reproduces the article's summary,
but that observation does not justify exclusion or identify a transcription
error. Conflicting specimen numbers also prevent silently joining all margin
records to fracture outcomes. Both fields remain unchanged. Seventy
below-simulated-bone face indicators describe 35 affected teeth, not 70
independent specimens. Conservative recorded-gap/later-fracture co-occurrence
bounds are 19/48–28/48; trusting all row codes gives 26/48. These are adverse
recorded outcomes in extracted restored teeth, not same-time events, fatigue
incidence, human prognosis or regenerative success.

The source gives a useful partial mechanical comparison with nominal geometry.
Measured repair depth, native donor identities, tracer leakage, per-specimen
fatigue history, pulp, living PDL and natural gum/position restoration remain
unavailable. The broader intake preserves these missing requirements.

**Aging:** 24 panel fits, 192 gene contributions, 192 gene omissions and 252
donor omissions preserve every specified result. No single-gene omission flips
the panel direction. The frozen common observed age span, 28–61 years,
removes every bulk donor older than 45; that bulk slope becomes positive.
The coarser shared-decade restriction retains older bulk donors and preserves
the negative direction. Exact donors/ages, model rank and leverage are retained.
Coarse shared bins do not establish matching ages, populations, sampling sites
or assays. This demonstrates support sensitivity, without identifying its
biological cause or resolving the original conflict.

**Rapamycin:** source pairing and choice of estimand explain the apparent
selectivity discrepancy. Three technical readings cannot turn four biological
experiments into twelve. Different C1/C2 assay states, unequal tissue pathway
responses and separate male blood/female tissue contexts remain explicit.
Normalized zeros do not establish absent kinase activity. Human transplant
observations and separate mouse reproductive findings supply no direct DL001
reproductive comparison, healthy-person testicular-volume estimate or matched
useful-aging-function result. Germline C1-dependent function also prevents
treating C2 sparing alone as sufficient protection.

## Next decisions

1. Use the real dental comparator to qualify specimen identity and adverse
   mechanical outcomes. The next discriminating repair input must add measured
   regional thickness on the intended substrate and valid same-unit histories.
   Native-like material must also preserve the remaining tooth under loading.
2. Seek matched-donor, qualified-site bulk and cell-resolved oral data with
   functional endpoints. Do not choose genes or an age restriction to obtain
   a preferred youthful direction.
3. Specify the useful tissue function a rapamycin comparator must retain, then
   require relevant-cell exposure and direct reproductive measurements in a
   comparable context. Current data cannot calibrate testis-sparing delivery
   or rank a new molecule.
4. Keep the whole restoration contract: original enamel/dentin substance and
   architecture, viable pulp/sensation, bilateral PDL/cementum/socket attachment,
   youthful gums, intended alignment and stable function after finite support
   withdrawal. The wider stem-cell, organoid, genome-repair and durable youthful
   body-function goals remain in the [continuity record](../restoration-run-plan-2026-10-11/GOALS_AND_CONTINUITY.md).

models, synthetic examples and the persistent goals. It is explicitly curated;
the desk does not fetch sources or run an experiment when a card is opened.

Public reports and selected text results are pinned by canonical size/hash;
raw caches and personal observations are not served by this feature.

An altered file withholds its whole card, including conclusion-bearing titles.
Failed refreshes clear earlier findings; superseded requests cannot restore an
obsolete result. Downloads recheck every artifact belonging to the card. File
matching is an integrity check, not confirmation of biology or source accuracy.
