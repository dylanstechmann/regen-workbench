# Rapamycin selectivity and reproductive protection: exploratory analysis plan

Written after inspecting the primary sources and before the final calculation. This is an exploratory reanalysis, not a preregistration, new compound, regimen, or reproductive-safety test.

The prior atlas already reviewed DL001, binary brain/periphery pharmacology, local skin delivery, human transplant reproductive associations, mouse recovery and the need to measure cell-specific exposure. The new question is whether actual source numbers strengthen a reproductive-protection comparison or only strengthen a pathway-selectivity comparison.

1. Reconstruct the four biological-replicate IC50 pairs in the original DL001 Figure 1f workbook. Convert the source scale consistently, retain C1/C2 pairing within compound, and distinguish mean of ratios from ratio of means. Do not count the three technical readings per experiment as independent biological replicates. Do not refit concentration curves or infer testicular potency from PC3 cells.
2. Reconstruct selected Figure 3d liver, gastrocnemius and heart pathway measurements and Figure S4 whole-blood parent measurements, preserving separate sex, cohort, duration, sampling and normalization context. Pathway measurements are not parent tissue exposure. Whole blood is not unbound plasma or intracellular concentration. Do not pair unrelated animals or claim matched pathway/parent exposure across cohorts.
3. Keep adult-mouse testis mass, histology, sperm measures, pathway effects and recovery separate. Use explicit source-reported numbers rather than invent individual observations. A weight change alone cannot establish reproductive function, and animal weight effects cannot establish human testicular shrinkage.
4. Keep the human transplant cohort descriptive. The abstract's 95 respondents are not established as the semen-analysis sample size. Retain group means, reported dispersion, pregnancy rates and their source CIs without deriving individual infertility risk, an adjusted effect, or human reversibility probabilities.
5. Use the primary Raptor germline genetic result as a mechanism counterexample to treating mTORC2 sparing as sufficient protection. Genetic deletion is not a quantitative drug threshold or equivalence model.
6. Produce a joint-evidence availability matrix and executable transport gates. A cell-selectivity ratio cannot fill absent native testicular exposure, reproductive endpoints, matched intended function, oral exposure or human data. No safe numerical threshold or candidate recommendation will be invented.
7. Pin downloaded bytes and actual `regen.record` receipts. The large original XLSX contains images; parse only required worksheet/shared-string XML, leaving molecular depictions and methods unused. Record that an initial bounded Range request returned only a prefix and was not a valid complete workbook. Use canonical UTF-8/LF input hashes for deterministic reproduction.

Priority decision: compare an already published selective candidate against rapamycin only after retaining a specified intended function and measuring reproductive endpoints and cell-specific parent/pathway exposure in the same relevant context. Selectivity alone can prioritize a mechanism for investigation; it cannot demonstrate reproductive protection.
