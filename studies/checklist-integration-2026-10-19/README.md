# Which repair outcomes are observed together?

This follow-up moves the [persistent restoration goals](../restoration-run-plan-2026-10-11/GOALS_AND_CONTINUITY.md)
toward linked functional observations. It examines three separate evidence
contexts: a restored-tooth interface after laboratory aging, human dental-pulp
repair observations and a clinical report of lesion-targeted base editing. Their populations,
units and endpoints remain separate. They cannot be combined into an overall
regeneration percentage.

## Executed observations

The [material source](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315036)
analysis retains all 200 OCT source positions after thermal
cycling. CHX-minus-control mean differences point in opposite directions
across the two adhesive modes. These are differences in an interface-signal
proxy, not measured repair-depth effects, a mechanical-fatigue result or
independent clinical effects. Worksheet-block nesting remains conditional.

In the [five-patient pulp pilot](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13287-017-0506-5),
four patients have positive EPT status at
24 weeks. Three have both positive EPT and unambiguously absent periapical
radiolucency at that visit. Patient 4's positive EPT coexists with a new
radiolucency; patient 2's late response does not change the recorded 24-week
negative EPT. MRI signal, CBCT mineralization and sensibility remain different
observations. This count describes a recorded pattern, not a pulp-regeneration
success rate or a controlled treatment effect.

The [original CPS1 clinical report](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2504747)
supplies ammonia medians of 23, 9 and
13 micromoles/L across its three stated windows. A later window's median is
higher than the intermediate window's median; these data do not define a
monotonic intervention-response curve. Concurrent care and different sampling
windows remain visible. Liver biopsy was deferred, so the patient's corrected
allele and hepatocyte fractions remain unmeasured here. The analysis does not
recover raw time series from printed summaries.

## Engineering decisions this evidence should change

**Lost tooth substance and an attachment deficit require different physical
measurements.** Preserve an individual's qualified reference geometry, then
keep restored enamel/dentin volume, host junction and cyclic behavior linked
on each research specimen. An interface observation in an established
restorative material does not supply a natural-enamel growth rate or original
thickness. The earlier [linkage packet](../checklist-mechanisms-2026-10-19/dental/REPORT.md)
retains the specimen/tooth/donor hierarchy needed for eligible records.

**Pulp repair requires discordant outcomes to remain visible.** A source can
report a positive sensory test and still report an adverse structural finding.
Analyze each endpoint at its recorded time, retain pre-existing disease and
new findings separately, and calculate overlap only where patient/time links
are explicit. Do not replace an unmeasured function with an imaging signal or
merge a later response into an earlier visit. An exploratory imaging cutoff
would need its own label and cannot become a healthy-tissue acceptance rule.

**Genome correction begins with a causal lesion and a relevant cell
population.** A functional response after an intervention targeting a named
monogenic lesion does not identify which ordinary-aging variants to correct. Keep endogenous
organ reserve, concurrent care, tissue sampling and follow-up windows visible.
A blood or functional observation cannot supply an unmeasured fraction of
corrected cells throughout an organ, much less throughout the body.

## Complete restoration remains the target

The tooth goal includes original substance, thickness, architecture and form;
living pulp and sensation; cementum, PDL, socket, gums and barrier; intended
position and bite; physiological mobility and durability after finite support
withdrawal. Different studies contributing different component measurements
do not establish that one tooth meets this full target.

Whole-body durable youthful function, organoids, stem cells and the public
base-editing project remain in scope. The earlier
[functional benchmarks](../checklist-function-2026-10-19/README.md) retain
MOTS-c and attachment/material-mechanics work; the
[source-linked analyses](../checklist-translation-2026-10-19/README.md) retain
gingival-age and rapamycin results. Other compound questions, including SS-31,
oral delivery, caffeine reversal and peptide-modification precedents, remain
in the continuity record. No complete restoration treatment or universal
rejuvenation intervention is established by this batch.
