## What remains connected to the original goals

- **Natural-tooth restoration:** enamel/dentin thickness, volume, architecture,
  interfaces and crack integrity; gums, PDL, socket, pulp and sensation remain
  required alongside original form and alignment. A thin layer or correct
  contour cannot complete the target.
- **Freedom from lifelong retention:** final-crown function and position after
  actual finite support withdrawal remain required. Neither the moment probes
  nor an in-vitro construct establishes this durability.
- **Individual pre-damage baseline:** the existing provenance/confidence map
  remains required. No new personal photographs or claims of exact hidden
  original anatomy were introduced in this run.
- **Whole-body youthful function:** the donor analysis challenges molecular
  readouts; it does not measure functional rejuvenation. Organ-specific repair,
  identity and durability must be evaluated separately.
- **Genome repair and organoids:** the public base-editing project was read
  for continuity. Its denominator work is retained; the new workbench audit
  concerns same-construct/same-region endpoint overlap. No editing target,
  guide or organoid treatment protocol was designed.
- **Earlier compounds:** oral delivery, useful persistence, manufacturing
  cost, peptide-modification precedents, caffeine reversal and rapamycin
  reproductive selectivity remain in the decision cards. Unverified structures,
  exposure values and human transfer remain unresolved rather than invented.
