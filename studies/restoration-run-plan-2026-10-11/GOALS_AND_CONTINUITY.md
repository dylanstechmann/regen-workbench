## End goals that must survive narrower experiments

1. **Restore the individual's natural teeth and the complete surrounding
   apparatus.** Recover lost enamel/dentin volume, original thickness,
   architecture and contours; intact interfaces and cracks; viable pulp and
   sensation; cementum, PDL, socket, gums, papillae and barrier; intended
   alignment, bite and physiological mobility. Damage may include impact,
   cracking, drilling, laser or thermal injury and prior restorative changes.
   Record reported mechanisms separately from measured damage.
2. **Restore stable position and function after finite support ends.** The
   desired endpoint is freedom from lifelong braces/retainers or other
   permanent external retention. Firm attachment in the wrong position, fusion
   to bone or recurring drift does not meet this goal. Temporary support is a
   research variable whose withdrawal requires follow-up, not success by itself.
3. **Use the person's undamaged baseline when available.** Earlier photos,
   scans, casts and surviving anatomy should constrain the individual target.
   Keep measured, image-constrained and inferred regions distinct. Historical
   photos can help recover appearance and visible form; they do not uniquely
   establish hidden thickness, internal anatomy or crack depth.
4. **Pursue durable youthful function throughout the body.** The aspiration
   includes every body component, rather than stopping at dental repair or a
   better biological-age score. Preserve personal anatomy, specialized cell
   identity and useful acquired information. Evaluate functional repair,
   integration and durability separately in each tissue; a local marker or
   geometry result is not whole-body rejuvenation.
5. **Continue stem-cell, organoid, engineered-tissue and genome-repair work.**
   Retain perfusion, innervation, adult identity, mechanical/physiological
   integration, genomic integrity and proliferation control as requirements.
   Keep the public `base-editing-evidence` project in scope; its name and public
   visibility were explicitly chosen by the owner. Do not merge separate
   methods repositories or claim their current status without checking them.
