# ASC+PRP periodontal trial: endpoint review

This is an attributed, source-scoped summary of Tobita et al. (2026), a
multicenter, open-label, randomized phase II comparison of autologous expanded
adipose-derived stromal cells plus platelet-rich plasma (ASC+PRP) with enamel
matrix derivative (EMD) in adult intrabony periodontal defects. It is not a
meta-analysis, clinical recommendation, or evidence of whole-tooth replacement.
The [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12855574/)
reports a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license; this page paraphrases the findings and does
not reproduce its tables or extended passages.

## Findings by endpoint

| Endpoint | Reported comparison | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: radiographic alveolar bone height at 36 weeks | ASC+PRP 4.010 ± 1.399 mm (n=8), EMD 2.105 ± 1.129 mm (n=6); difference 1.905 mm (95% CI 0.383–3.427), p=.0184 | A radiographic bone-height signal. It does not establish oriented periodontal ligament attachment or functional load transfer. |
| Clinical attachment level and probing depth | Main text reports no significant between-group difference at 12, 24 or 36 weeks. The ASC+PRP arm improved from baseline in CAL (within-arm p<.01). | Within-arm change is not an added treatment effect over EMD. Exact numeric secondary contrasts are not reproduced because the supplementary endpoint tables were not accessible in this review. |
| Bone width | No significant difference; reported values 1.355 ± 1.938 versus 0.750 ± 1.300 mm, p=.5227 | Secondary radiographic measure; do not combine with the primary height result. |
| Safety | One AE and one SAE in the ASC+PRP arm; acute periodontitis and heart failure occurred in two different participants. Authors judged both unrelated to the procedure. | The reported confidence interval is wide (11.1%, 95% CI 0.3–48.2% for each event category); this small cohort cannot establish rare-event safety. |

The [official jRCT entry](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/latest-detail/jRCTb030190173)
reports 16 participants consented, one withdrew before treatment, and 15
received treatment (9 ASC+PRP and 6 EMD). The primary 36-week analysis includes
8 ASC+PRP and 6 EMD participants. The retrieved article and registry do not
explain why one treated participant is absent from the primary endpoint count;
no cause is inferred here.

## What the study tested

The intervention combined approximately 15 million expanded autologous adipose
stromal cells with PRP gel. There was no ASC-only or PRP-only group, so the
contribution of either component cannot be isolated. These are adipose-derived
cells, not periodontal-ligament stem cells (PDLSCs). Treatment of an existing
periodontal defect also does not test de novo whole-tooth formation, pulp
integration, gingival barrier restoration, or long-term occlusal function.

The article describes the trial as a therapeutic-equivalence study. Its Methods
report sample-size planning around a hypothesized bone-height difference and
ordinary two-sided Student t-tests, with no stated equivalence margin or formal
equivalence procedure. Therefore this review treats the null clinical
attachment/probing-depth comparisons as “no significant between-group signal,”
not proof of equivalence. The planned 10 participants per arm were not reached;
the observed endpoint standard deviations were also larger than the .6 mm value
used in the sample-size assumption. The article reports no multiplicity
adjustment across repeated timepoints.

No histology was obtained. Thus the study cannot show oriented cementum–PDL–bone
insertion, physiological ligament function, or absence of ankylosis. Bone height
and clinical attachment remain separate clinical proxies.

The next discriminating evidence would be a prespecified, adequately powered
comparison with a declared clinical attachment endpoint and equivalence or
superiority estimand, participant-level flow for each endpoint, multiplicity
handling, and longer follow-up. For a tooth-regeneration claim, separate
histology and functional loading evidence would still be required for oriented
cementum–ligament–bone integration, alongside pulp, gingival barrier and whole
organ integration measures.
