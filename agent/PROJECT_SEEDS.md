Do not invent ten new repos. The methods packages already exist as siblings
under the parent workspace. Pick one row. Run tests in that repo. Stop.

1. **Hypothesis chemistry (atlas)**
   Repo: `geroscience-compound-atlas`
   Command: `make hypothesis`
   Output: `hypotheses/YYYY-MM-DD-mtor-hypothesis/`
   Not: a stack, a dose, or play-mode monsters filed as cards.

   `Vmax` as measured uptake.

   The NIST three-well case is already the real table. Do not train on
   the synthetic generator and call it iPSC QC.

   Repos: `diffmedia-loop`, `cell-protocol-compiler`
   Propose only points the compiler would accept.

   Balanced OT only. The unidentifiable-branch fixture is a feature.

6. **Pump math vs balance trace**
   Repos: `open-perfusion-rig`, `perfusion-calibration-lab`
   Do not stream firmware scripts. Do not claim a physical calibration
   that has not been measured.
